How it works

Three steps from the car park to the stamp

  1. Visit a park

    Pick a park from the stamp index. Each one has its own two pages with things to do and what is on nearby.

  2. Check in

    Add the date and a photo. Tick what you did, tag who came along and write a few lines to remember the day.

  3. Get the stamp

    The stamp is inked on the park page and in the index. Some visits also unlock a secret stamp.

The booklet

Eight regions, eight inks

Every region stamps in its own colour, so a full passport reads like a map of where you have been.

Features

A keepsake first, an app second

Park pages

One spread for every park

The left page shows the place. The right page is yours: stamp, date, your photo, who you went with and your memories in handwriting.

Secret stamps

Hidden stamps for the curious

See glow-worms after dark at Springbrook, arrive in whale season at K'gari, or finish a whole region page. Each one unlocks a stamp you cannot see in advance.

Friends

Tag who came along

Type @username in the "went with" line. Your friend gets the same park stamp in their own passport.

Privacy

Public only when you say so

Every visit is private by default. Tick one box to share it on the community feed, and untick it any time.

Community

See where others have been

The feed shows shared visits with the stamp, photo and note. Tap a name to see that traveller's posts.

Milestones

Explorer, Trailblazer, Grand Traveller

Special stamps at 10 parks, 25 parks and a finished passport.
